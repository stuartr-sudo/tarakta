## The reversal hypothesis ("what if we flipped every trade?")

Stuart's question 2026-08-01: ~"If we simply reversed all the trades we'd be significantly more profitable — why are we wrong 85% of the time?"

Tested with `scripts/replay_reversal_test.py`: every signal re-simulated BOTH directions under identical symmetric management (−1R stop, +2R target, mirrored stop distance, 168h timeout, both-touched-in-bar counts as loss), against real Binance 1h candles. Same ruler both sides → isolates directional edge from exit management.

### Raw result (looks like the hypothesis is right)

| | Total R | Win rate |
|---|---|---|
| Original direction, 2R sim | −12.3R | 24% (9/38) |
| Reversed direction, 2R sim | +15.9R | 50% (19/38) |

### Deduped result (the raw result is an illusion)

Collapsing same-symbol re-entries <24h apart into one *idea* (BTC Jul 20–21 = 7 consecutive shorts of one idea; DOGE Apr 22 = 3; ETH Jun 15 = 2, Jul 13 = 3; ADA Feb 14 = 2) → 28 independent ideas:

| Per independent idea | Total R |
|---|---|
| Original direction | ≈ −0.3R (coin flip) |
| Reversed direction | ≈ +0.9R (coin flip) |

Avg market drift after entry, signed toward the original trade: +0.26%/24h — noise.

**Conclusion: the engine is NOT an anti-oracle.** Per idea its entries have ~zero edge in either direction. The live inverse-mirror bot (`tarakta-mm-inverse`) losing −$1.1k confirms this out-of-sample. Do NOT build an inverse strategy.

## Why 76–85% of trades lose — three stacked mechanisms, ranked

1. **Serial re-entry martingale on losers (~8R of the −8.7R).** A winner occupies the position slot for days → the idea trades once. A loser is stopped in 1–3h → slot frees, formation still on the chart → the engine re-enters the same wrong idea 3–7×. Losing ideas are oversampled; winning ideas are not. This converts coin-flip entries into a 76% loss rate. Fix candidate: post-stop-loss re-entry cooldown per symbol+setup — **find the course's own re-entry guidance and cite lesson+timestamp before shipping** (CLAUDE.md rule). Note the committee already provides this defense in practice (it vetoed all 7 July BTC re-entries in shadow); veto mode is gated on the §5 scoreboard in LAUNCH_HANDOFF.
2. **Exit asymmetry.** Only 1/38 full win vs 24/38 full-loss; the 30/40/30 ladder + breakeven-after-TP1 caps winners while losers pay full −1R. (The engine's scratch rules DO help defensively: engine management −8.7R beat naive 2R management's −12.3R on the same signals.) Fix: exit-ladder course-fidelity audit — does the progression match the lesson's target hierarchy?
3. **Shape without location (the known root cause, reconfirmed).** Only 4/43 historical candidates at course-valid locations (vision audit). 30/38 signals short, mostly shorting thrice-hit levels mid-structure — absorption as often as reversal. Without location, pattern grammar carries no information: exactly what "≈0R both directions" means. Fix: the location-first nominator (build queue #2).
